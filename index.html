Plants vs. Zombies fan clone

Click a seed packet (or press 1–7), then click a lawn tile. Click falling suns to collect them. Iceberg Lettuce freezes the zombie that bites it. The Potato Mine arms after 10 seconds, then blows up under the next zombie that steps on it (kills normal and cone zombies, not bucketheads). The Cabbage-pult lobs cabbages at the nearest zombie in its lane. S shovel · P pause · Esc cancel

Merge guide (unlocks in level 2)

Level 2 — two normal plants: Peashooter + Iceberg = Snow Pea · Peashooter + Peashooter = Repeater (double shot) · Peashooter + Wall-nut = Pea-nut (tanky shooter) · Peashooter + Sunflower = Sun Pea (+10 sun per shot) · Sunflower + Sunflower = Duo Flower (50-sun drops) · Wall-nut + Wall-nut = Tall-nut (double health) · Iceberg + Iceberg = Freezer Salad (freezes a 3×3 area for twice as long).

Level 3 — merged plants too: Peashooter + Repeater = Threepeater (3 peas down its lane; click it to switch to 1 pea in its lane + 1 in each neighbouring lane, click again to switch back).
