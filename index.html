Plants vs. Zombies fan clone

Click a seed packet (or press 1–5), then click a lawn tile. Click falling suns to collect them. Iceberg Lettuce freezes the zombie that bites it — or drop it on a Peashooter (or vice versa) to merge them into a Snow Pea. Other Peashooter merges: + Peashooter = Repeater (double shot), + Wall-nut = Pea-nut (tanky shooter), + Sunflower = Sun Pea (+10 sun per shot). Peashooter + Repeater = Threepeater (3 peas down its lane; click it to switch to 1 pea in its lane + 1 in each neighbouring lane, click again to switch back). S shovel · P pause · Esc cancel
